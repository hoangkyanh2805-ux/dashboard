# Azzam Trading Brand Guide

This dashboard follows `channels/SPEC_AZZAM.md`, the Azzam brand reference available in this repository.

## Visual identity
- Background: `#020712`; panels: `rgba(4, 15, 31, .84)` and `rgba(6, 22, 45, .72)`.
- Main accents: blue `#13a8ff`, cyan `#3fe8ff`, and gold `#ffd15a`.
- Positive results: green `#32d583`; negative results: red `#ff4d5f`.
- Main text: `#f4f8ff`; muted text: `#8da1b8`.
- Use Oswald for headings and numbers, matching the bold condensed lettering in `assets/azam-brand.jpg`. Use Inter for body copy, controls, and labels.
- Logo: `assets/azam-logo.jpg`. Brand reference: `assets/azam-brand.jpg`.

## Layout and behavior
- Keep Tom's original recap layout and editing flow. Apply Azzam's logo and typography without redesigning the template.
- Adapt Tom's weekly recap layout for Azzam: five metric cards, daily trade breakdown, result summary, and brand footer.
- Use Azzam metrics and trade fields: net profit, pips, win rate, profit factor, trade count; date, profit, pips, signals, notes.
- Responsive breakpoints: 980px and 640px.
- Storage key: `azzam-trading-performance-v1`.
- PNG filename: `azzam-trading-performance-{YYYY-MM-DD}.png`.
